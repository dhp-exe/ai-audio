The problem

Most micro-dramas vanish. Series with recurring stars don't.

Short drama is one of the fastest-growing entertainment formats in Asia, but hits are rare and live-action production is slow and expensive. The data points one way: audiences come back for characters they already know.

Recurring characters are what audiences return for, and they build a fan base that carries over.

Audiences come back for characters.

So we build the characters first, and test every story in audio before it gets a picture.

Our solution

An agent team for production. Character IPs for the audience.

Agents do the production work at the speed and cost of software; Character IPs give audiences someone to come back for. Every story is proven in audio first, then the winners move to the short-drama pipeline with the same agents and the same characters.

What sets us apart

Two things other tools don't have together.

Voice tools give you a voice. Video generators give you a clip. Emvoox gives you a production team of agents and a cast of characters that stays with you from one series to the next.

Differentiator 01

An agentic production pipeline

Not a single generator: a team of specialist agents, each with its own skills and a validated handoff, coordinated by a Producer you talk to. It plans, delegates, checks its own work and stops for your decisions.

  • Research, writing, casting, direction, voice, QA and release in one run
  • Scenes produced in parallel, each with its own QA and retries
  • Any step re-runs alone; nothing is paid for twice
Differentiator 02

Character IPs with a voice and a look

Our characters are owned assets, not one-off prompts. Each Character IP has a locked persona, voice and visual identity, plays many roles across series, and builds a fan base that every new story inherits.

  • One IP per role, changed only through an audited unlock
  • Emotion performed inside each IP's identity
  • The same face and voice carry into the short-drama pipeline

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What makes Emvoox different from AI voice or video tools?

Two things together. A pipeline of specialist agents that produces a whole series, from research to release, with QA and a human gate; and Character IPs that keep the same voice and look in every series, so audiences follow them.

What is a Character IP?

A reusable character-actor with a locked persona, voice and visual identity, who plays many roles across series. The role changes from story to story; the IP stays the same, and so does its fan base.

Is Emvoox only for audio?

No. Audio is where every story is tested first, because it is the cheapest way to learn whether it holds an audience. The short-drama pipeline, audio plus images with the same Character IPs, is next on our roadmap.

Is the content made by AI?

The agents do most of the production, directed and reviewed by people. A person approves every episode before release, and AI use is disclosed according to each platform's policy.

Can you produce a series from my story?

Yes, that is our production service. Send us the story or a brief; we agree scope, cost and a reviewer, then deliver approved episodes.

How is Emvoox related to V1RON?

Emvoox runs as a domain on V1RON OS, an AI operating system on owned hardware. V1RON provides the runtime, model access, storage and security; Emvoox owns the agent team, the Character IPs and the content.

Can I license a Character IP or use the pipeline for my team?

Character IP licensing and pipeline access for partners are on our roadmap. Tell us what you need and we will include you in the early partner group.
